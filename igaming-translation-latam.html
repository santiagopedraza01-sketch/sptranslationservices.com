Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is one “neutral” Spanish enough for all of Latin America?

For help centres and legal text, a neutral Latin American Spanish often works well. For marketing, sportsbook and promotions, adapting to each country usually performs better, because vocabulary, currencies and favourite sports differ.

Which languages do you work in for iGaming?

For now we focus on Spanish, for Spain and every Spanish-speaking market in Latin America, translating from English. That way every project is handled by native speakers who know the sector.

Can you handle urgent launches?

Yes. Tell us your go-live date and we plan the work in batches so the most important pages are delivered first.
