About SP Translations

A Malta-based translation agency founded by Santiago Pedraza Castillo, providing certified English to Spanish translation and professional translation services in 120+ languages.

Who we are

Translation with a name and a signature behind it.

Accurate translations, signed by the people who make them.

SP Translations is a translation agency based in St Paul’s Bay, Malta. It was founded by Santiago Pedraza Castillo, an MFEA-recognised translator with an MA in Translation and Terminology Studies from the University of Malta, who personally handles English ⇄ Spanish projects.

We translate official, legal, academic and business documents for individuals and companies: from birth certificates and university transcripts to contracts, court documents and the day-to-day paperwork of international teams, including Malta’s iGaming sector. For other languages, we work with a professional network of specialised translators covering more than 120 languages.

Every certified translation identifies the translator responsible and comes with a signed certificate of accuracy, so you always know who stands behind your document.

How we work

The standards behind every project.

What you can expect from us.

I

Accuracy

Names, dates, stamps, figures and official titles are checked against the original before anything is signed.

II

Accountability

The translator who does the work signs the certificate of accuracy, with their name and qualifications on the document.

III

Confidentiality

Documents are only shared with the professionals needed to complete your project, and NDAs are available on request.

Fast, clear and online. Standard documents in 24–48 hours, a fixed written quotation before any work starts, and support 24/7 by WhatsApp, phone or email.
