Certified translations in Malta

Official translations of certificates, diplomas, court papers and company documents, signed by a recognised translator and ready to submit in Malta or abroad. Fully online, from €39 per page.

Which translation?

Three levels, depending on where the document goes.

Which translation do I need?

For information

Standard translation

An accurate translation for your own understanding, internal use or communication with a company.

  • Not signed or certified
  • Emails, letters, internal documents
  • Lowest cost
For Maltese authorities

Certified translation

An official translation issued, signed, dated and stamped by a professional, accredited translator.

  • Public Registry, JobsPlus, courts, banks
  • Employers and universities
  • Delivered as a signed PDF
For use abroad

Certified + apostille

When a document has to be presented in another country, an apostille may be needed on the original, the translation or both.

  • Hague Convention countries
  • We check the destination’s rules first
  • Sworn translations for Spain: ask us

Not sure which one you need? Read sworn vs certified translation, or ask us before you order.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Will a Maltese authority accept the translation?

Each official translation is signed, dated and stamped by a professional, accredited translator, which is what Maltese institutions normally ask for. If an institution has specific requirements, we check them before preparing the translation.

Do I need to send the original document?

Usually not. A clear scan or photograph is enough when all text, stamps and seals are legible. If an institution asks for a hard copy, we can post it.

Do you translate the apostille as well?

Yes. If your original document already carries an apostille, it is translated together with the document, so the authority receives the full content in English.

How fast can I get it?

Standard documents are delivered in 24–48 hours. Urgent same-day or next-morning delivery is available for a 40% supplement, subject to availability.
