Getting it right

So your documents match.

What matters in an Italian translation.

I

Names exactly as in your passport

Names, places and dates are copied exactly as they appear in your passport or ID, so every document in your application matches.

II

Stamps, seals and handwriting

Official stamps, seals, signatures and handwritten notes are described and translated, so nothing on the original is left out.

III

Extracts and full copies

Italian civil records are issued as certificates, extracts (estratto) or full copies (copia integrale). We translate the version you have; check which one your institution asks for.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you translate both Italian to English and English to Italian?

Yes. We translate Italian into English and English into Italian. Translations are carried out by professional native-speaker translators, and every official translation is signed, dated and stamped by a professional, accredited translator.

Will my Italian translation be accepted in Malta?

Official translations are signed, dated and stamped by a professional, accredited translator, which is what most authorities, employers, banks and universities ask for. If an institution has its own requirements, send them with your documents and we prepare the translation accordingly.

Does a document from Italy need an apostille?

Italy is a party to the Hague Apostille Convention, so a public document from Italy that will be used in Malta is normally legalised with an apostille issued in Italy. Check the receiving authority’s requirements; we translate the apostille too.

How much does an Italian translation cost, and how long does it take?

Every document is quoted individually, based on its length, layout and language pair, and the delivery date is confirmed in your quotation before you pay. Priority service is available, subject to availability.
