Cookie policy

How sptranslationservices.com uses cookies and similar technologies, and how you can control them. Last updated: 10 October 2026.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as your browser’s local storage, work in the same way. They let a site remember information between visits, for example your settings.

What we use

This website does not use tracking, analytics or advertising cookies. It only stores the following items in your browser’s local storage. They are never sent to our servers.

NamePurposeCategoryDuration
sp-consentRemembers your cookie choices.Necessary12 months
fx-currencyRemembers the currency you chose in the fees section.PreferencesUntil you clear it or withdraw consent
wa-card-dismissedRemembers that you closed the WhatsApp card.PreferencesUntil you clear it or withdraw consent

Preference items are only stored if you allow the “Preferences” category. If you withdraw consent, we delete them.

Analytics and marketing

We do not currently use analytics or marketing cookies. If we add them in the future, we will update this policy, and they will only run after you allow the relevant category.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts — the site’s fonts are loaded from Google’s servers, which receive your IP address to deliver them. No cookies are set for this.
  • Stripe — when you pay an invoice you are taken to a Stripe payment page, where Stripe’s own cookie and privacy policies apply.
  • FormSubmit — processes the quote and review forms and forwards them to us by email. It does not set cookies on this site.
  • Google Maps, LinkedIn and WhatsApp — only contacted if you follow a link to them; their own policies then apply.
