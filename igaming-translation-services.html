Localisation

Not word for word.

Content that reads as if it was written for each market.

A literal translation of a casino or sportsbook loses players: the wrong betting term, a currency in the wrong format or a bonus headline that falls flat tells them the brand was not made for them. Localisation adapts the whole experience, so it feels local and stays compliant.

  • —
    Currency, number and date formats
  • —
    Odds formats: decimal, fractional and American
  • —

  • —
    Sports, leagues and cultural references
  • —
    Responsible gambling helplines and age wording
  • —
    Tone and form of address (tú, usted or vos in Spanish)
  • —
    Length of buttons, banners and push notifications

Transcreation and multilingual SEO

Process

Built around release dates.

From brief to go-live.

  1. 01

    Brief and files

    Send an export or a sample, your target markets and your deadline. We confirm word count, languages and a fixed quote, usually within a few working hours.

  2. 02

    Glossary and style

    We agree brand terms, game names that stay in English, tone and form of address before translation starts.

  3. 03

    Translation and localisation

    Native translators with iGaming experience translate and adapt the content for each market.

  4. 04

    Review and checks

    A professional review covers terminology, numbers, odds, placeholders and length limits. With access to staging, we also check the text in context.

  5. 05

    Delivery and updates

    Files come back ready to import. Weekly promotions and updates reuse your glossary and previous translations, so repeated text costs less.

Urgent launch or promotion? Short updates can be turned around the same day or overnight with priority service, subject to availability, and weekend cover is available by arrangement. Larger launches can be delivered in stages, so priority pages go live first.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which languages do you cover for iGaming?

All our core languages: English, Spanish, Italian, French, German, Polish, Romanian, Russian, Portuguese, Turkish, Greek, Dutch, Serbian and Maltese. Spanish is adapted for Spain or for each Latin American market, and more languages are available through our translator network. Every language is handled by native translators who know the sector.

Can you work with our CMS or translation files?

Yes. We can work with exported files such as XLIFF, JSON, CSV, Excel or Word, keeping placeholders, variables and tags intact so they can be imported back.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes. NDAs are available on request, and your content is only shared with the translators working on your project.

How do you price iGaming projects?

Usually per word, with discounts for repeated and updated content. Send us a sample or a file export and we quote within a few working hours.

How fast can you turn around iGaming content?

Short promotions and compliance updates can often be delivered the same day or overnight with priority service, subject to availability. For larger launches we agree a schedule in the quote and can deliver in stages, so the most important pages go live first.

What is the difference between translation and transcreation?

Translation keeps the meaning of the original. Transcreation rewrites slogans, bonus headlines and campaigns for the new audience, keeping the intent and the offer rather than the exact words. It suits marketing; terms and conditions and game rules are translated precisely.

Do you translate SEO keywords for casino and betting sites?

Yes. We research the terms players search in each market and country and work them into titles, meta descriptions, headings and copy, instead of translating your English keywords literally.

Do you translate game rules and paytables for studios?

Yes. We translate rules, paytables, help screens and feature descriptions, keeping values, symbols and placeholders exactly as they are in your files.

Do you know the rules of each regulated market?

We translate using the terminology and conventions each regulator and market expects, and flag wording that usually needs a local version. We are translators, not lawyers, so the final legal review stays with your compliance team or local counsel.
