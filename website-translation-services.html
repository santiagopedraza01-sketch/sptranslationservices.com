Multilingual SEO

Translate what people actually search for.

Found in every language.

A literal translation of your keywords is often not what people type. “Online casino” is casino online in some countries and casino en línea in others; “certified translation” is searched differently in Spain and in Mexico.

When you need it, we research the terms used in each target market and use them naturally in titles, headings, meta descriptions and body copy, without stuffing keywords.

  • Keyword research per language and country
  • Translated titles, meta descriptions and URLs
  • Alt text, structured data and hreflang guidance

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How is website translation priced?

Per word, based on the language pair and the type of content. Repeated text, such as menus and footers, is only counted once. Send us your URL list or export for a fixed quotation.

Can you translate directly in our CMS?

Usually we work on exported files so nothing breaks, but we can also work in your CMS or staging site if you give us access.

Do you only proofread, without translating?

Yes. We proofread and edit existing translations and content written by non-native speakers, with tracked changes so you can see every correction.
