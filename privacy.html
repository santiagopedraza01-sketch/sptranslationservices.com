Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026.

1. Who we are

SP Translations (“we”, “us”) is a translation agency run by Santiago Pedraza Castillo, Triq It-Trill, St Paul’s Bay SPB 1674, Malta. We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy. Contact: santiagopedraza01@gmail.com · +356 79066398.

2. What data we collect

  • Quote requests: your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number (optional), the service, languages, deadline and project details you give us, and any documents you upload.
  • Contact messages: your name, email address, phone number (optional) and your message.
  • Reviews: your name, email address, country (optional), rating and review text. Only your first name, the initial of your surname and your country are published.
  • Communications: what you send us by email, phone or WhatsApp.
  • Payments: when you pay an invoice, Stripe processes your card or wallet details. We receive confirmation of the payment, your name, email address, the amount and the invoice reference, but never your full card details.
  • Technical data: like any website, our hosting provider and font provider receive your IP address and basic browser information when you visit. See our cookie policy for what is stored in your browser.

The documents you send for translation may contain sensitive information, such as identity documents, medical reports or criminal records. We only use them to provide the translation you ask for.

3. Why we use it and our legal basis

  • To prepare quotations and deliver translations: to take steps at your request before a contract and to perform it (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Where documents contain special categories of data or criminal records, we process them with your explicit consent, given when you send them, and only as needed for your translation.
  • To answer your messages: our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To invoice and keep accounting records: to comply with our legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • To publish your review: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Optional cookies: your consent, managed through our cookie banner.

4. Who we share it with

We never sell your data. We only share it when needed:

  • Translators in our network, when a project needs another language or specialist. They are bound by confidentiality.
  • Service providers who process data on our behalf: FormSubmit (delivers website form submissions to our inbox), Google (email and website fonts), Stripe (payments), GitHub (website hosting) and WhatsApp/Meta (if you message us on WhatsApp).
  • Notaries, apostille offices or couriers, only if you ask us to arrange those services.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

5. International transfers

Some of these providers are based outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where data is transferred, it is protected by appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

6. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and quotations that do not lead to a project: up to 12 months.
  • Project documents and translations: up to 12 months after delivery, so we can provide copies if you need them, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Invoices and accounting records: for as long as Maltese tax and accounting law requires.
  • Published reviews: until you ask us to remove them.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to receive it in a portable format. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. To exercise any of these rights, email santiagopedraza01@gmail.com.

You also have the right to complain to the Information and Data Protection Commissioner in Malta (idpc.org.mt) or to the data protection authority in your own country.

8. Security

We protect your data with appropriate technical and organisational measures, and share documents only with the people who need them to complete your project. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please contact us if you would prefer to send particularly sensitive documents another way.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

10. Contact

For any question about your personal data, email santiagopedraza01@gmail.com.
