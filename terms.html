Quotations are free and without obligation. They are based on the documents and information you provide, and are valid for the period stated in the quotation. A project is confirmed when you accept the quotation and, unless we agree otherwise in writing, pay the agreed amount. Work begins once payment is received. Standard single-page documents are normally delivered within 24–48 hours; the delivery date for each project is stated in the quotation. We are not responsible for delays caused by incomplete documents, late information or third parties such as notaries, apostille offices or couriers.

5. Your responsibilities

  • Send clear, complete and legible copies, and tell us the correct spelling of names, places and other key details.
  • Tell us the requirements of the institution where the translation will be submitted. We can review them before preparing your translation.
  • Make sure you have the right to send us the documents for translation.

6. Certified translations

Each official translation is issued, signed, dated and stamped by a professional, accredited translator. We prepare translations according to the requirements you share with us, but the final decision to accept a document rests with the receiving institution, which we cannot control.

If you find an error that is our responsibility, tell us within a reasonable time after delivery and we will correct it free of charge. Changes to the source document or to your instructions after delivery may be charged as new work.
