Process

From brief to publication.

How a writing project works.

  1. 01

    Brief

    Tell us your audience, goals, tone, keywords and any examples you like. We confirm scope, price and delivery date.

  2. 02

    Outline

    For longer pieces we agree the structure and headings first, so the content goes in the right direction from the start.

  3. 03

    Writing

    The content is written from scratch by a native writer of each language, in your brand’s voice.

  4. 04

    Review and delivery

    We revise based on your feedback and deliver ready to publish, with headings, meta title and meta description where needed.

SEO

Found and read.

Written for people and for search engines.

Good content answers the question the reader typed. We research the terms people use in each market, which often differ from one language to another, and even between Spain and Latin America, and use them naturally in titles, headings and copy, without stuffing keywords.

Already have content in one language? We can also translate and localise it instead.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which languages do you write in?

All our core languages: English, Spanish, Italian, French, German, Polish, Romanian, Russian, Portuguese, Turkish, Greek, Dutch, Serbian and Maltese. For Spanish we write for Spain or for Latin America, or for a specific country if your audience is there.

Is the content original?

Yes. Every piece is written from scratch for your brief and is never copied from other sites.

Can you write the same content in several languages?

Yes. From one brief we write each version natively, so every language reads as an original rather than a translation, with the same message.

How is content writing priced?

Per word or per piece, depending on the project. Send us your brief and we confirm the price and delivery date in your quotation.
