Questions

Frequently asked questions

What files can you work with?

Word, Excel and PDF, or text exported from your exhibition design files. We return the translation ready for your designer and flag any line that runs longer than the space allows.

Do you translate audio guide scripts for recording?

Yes. We translate scripts to be spoken, keep each track close to its original length and can mark how names should be pronounced for the voice artist.

Can you translate an exhibition into several languages at once?

Yes. We coordinate all the languages from one set of source texts, so panels, labels and the audio guide say the same thing in every language.

How are museum projects priced?

Usually per word, quoted once we have seen the texts. Send a sample or the full list of panels and labels, and we confirm the price and delivery date in your quotation.
